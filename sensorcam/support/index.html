Support

Help with SensorCam RAW on iPhone.

Getting help

Write to akos@tallpoppystudio.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you were doing when something went wrong, and you will get a reply from a person, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

Which iPhones support open gate video? Open Gate, Open Gate Log and ProRes RAW use the full sensor readout that iPhone 17 Pro and later provide. Earlier iPhones record 4:3 Max and 4:3 Log instead. 48 MP ProRAW photos need an iPhone that supports ProRAW; other models save HEIF.

Why does ProRes RAW need a drive? iOS only lets apps record ProRes RAW to an external USB-C drive. This applies to every camera app. Without a drive, use Open Gate or Open Gate Log, which also use the full sensor and record to the iPhone in ProRes 422 HQ or 10-bit HEVC.

What's the difference between Open Gate and Open Gate Log? Open Gate records standard colour that looks right straight away. Open Gate Log records a flat log image that keeps more highlight and shadow detail for colour grading. You can import a .cube LUT in Settings to bake a look into Open Gate Log recordings.

Where are my photos and videos? In the Files app under On My iPhone → SensorCam RAW → Media, and in your photo library if "Save to Photos" is on.

How much space does open gate video take? About 8–10 GB a minute in ProRes 422 HQ and much less in HEVC. The status bar shows how many minutes are left.

Video records without sound. Another app or a call was using the microphone. End the call or close the other app, then reopen SensorCam RAW.

The phone gets warm. Full-sensor recording is demanding. If the iPhone gets too hot, iOS may pause the camera; let it cool down for a few minutes.
