Privacy Policy

Gaiwan keeps your tea records on your device. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Two features send data off your device, and both are described below.

Last updated 21 October 2026

What stays on your device

Your sessions, tasting notes, cabinet entries, teaware and photographs are stored locally on your iPhone. They are included in your device backup if you back your iPhone up, and they are deleted with the app if you remove it. iCloud sync is switched off in this release.

The Apple Watch app is a remote for the timer on your iPhone. The two talk to each other directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity, so the tea names and timer state the watch shows never pass through any server.

What leaves your device, and when

Two features make requests to Gaiwan's own server, which forwards the content to Google's Gemini API and returns the answer. The server does not keep the photos or the text it forwards.

Identifying a tea or a piece of teaware. This runs only when you choose photos and tap Identify. The photos you chose are sent, with anything you typed into the hint field and the language your app is set to.

Written summaries (subscribers only). When you open a tea's card and its summary is missing or out of date, Gaiwan sends what the summary is written from: that tea's card (its name, category, origin, cultivar, harvest, vendor, amounts and your notes about it), its entry in Gaiwan's built-in catalogue, up to 30 of your sessions with that tea (date, leaf, water, temperature, steep times, the vessel's name, your rating and your tasting notes) and one of its photos, reduced in size. Locations and place names are not sent. The finished summary is stored on your device.

With either request, the server also receives your anonymous subscription identifier (see below), to check with RevenueCat whether your subscription is active, and your IP address. Both are used to keep count of requests so the service cannot be abused; those counts are kept for at most a day and hold nothing else.

Google processes the forwarded content under its own terms. If you use neither feature, Gaiwan makes no network requests beyond the map tiles Apple Maps loads to draw a map and the subscription check described below.

Subscriptions

Identification beyond the free allowance, and written summaries, need a subscription, which is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our payments provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device and the fact that a purchase happened, so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. That identifier is not your name, your email or your Apple Account. It is a random string, and it is not connected to anything else about you.

Apple handles the payment itself. Gaiwan never sees your card details, and no payment information reaches us or our servers.

Location

Location is optional. It is requested only when you tap "Add current location" while starting a session, taken once at that moment, and used to place that session on your map. Gaiwan does not track you in the background and does not follow your movements. Declining the permission leaves every other part of the app working.

Camera and photo library

Gaiwan asks for the camera when you photograph a tea, its packaging or your teaware, and for your photo library when you attach an existing picture. It reads only the images you pick. Photos are stored with the record you attached them to, and leave the device only as described above.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers
  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no names, email addresses or contact details
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared beyond the services named above: Gaiwan's server (hosted by Vercel, with request counts kept by Upstash), Google for identification and summaries, and RevenueCat for knowing whether your subscription is active

Children

Gaiwan is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your device will be described in the app's release notes.
