Mobile apps

01

Native iOS and Android, from architecture to store release. Subscriptions, offline data, maps, health and connected hardware. Analytics and growth after launch.

Current client work

Pharmacist rewards platform

Pharmaceutical · under NDA

A sales-incentive product for a pharmaceutical company, built end to end: a pharmacist-facing mobile app that reads invoices with on-device capture and AI extraction to credit reward points, the backend that runs the points, catalogue and redemption logic, and a web dashboard the client uses to run campaigns and watch performance.

Android · iOS · Backend · Web dashboard · AI document extraction

Rider app

The first iOS app, built from zero and carried from pre-launch to millions of users as the mobility startup became European market leader. Real-time vehicle location and availability over MapKit, and expansion features across more than ten markets under hyper-growth.
