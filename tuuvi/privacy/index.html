Privacy Policy

Tuuvi keeps your family's profiles on your device and in your own iCloud account. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and it uses no third-party services. The only outside services involved are Apple's, described below.

Last updated 23 September 2026

What Tuuvi stores

For each person you add: a name, a birth date, a skin type, whether their skin is sensitive, an emoji and colour for their avatar, the SPF they use, and when sunscreen was last applied. Tuuvi also keeps a record of each application — the time, the UV index, the SPF, the activities you chose (water, sweat, sand, towels) and the name of the city you were in.

This data is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is switched on, Apple also keeps it in the private database of your iCloud account so it reaches your other devices. We have no access to that database and no copy of your data on any server of ours — we don't run one. Deleting the app removes the data from your device; you can remove the iCloud copy in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Children

Tuuvi is built for parents looking after their children's skin, so profiles often describe a child. Those profiles are created by the parent, stay on the parent's device and iCloud, and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Tuuvi does not collect personal information from children, and does not let anyone contact them.

Location

Tuuvi uses your location to fetch the UV forecast for where you are, and to show the name of your city. Your coordinates are sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the forecast and to Apple Maps to look up the city name; both are processed by Apple under its own privacy policy. Tuuvi may notice when you have moved a significant distance so the forecast stays local, but it does not record where you have been or keep a location history. You can decline the permission, but Tuuvi cannot give a forecast without it.

Health

With your permission, Tuuvi reads your Fitzpatrick skin type from the Health app so you don't have to enter it, and can show the UV exposure an Apple Watch has recorded for you. Health data is used only to show you information and make recommendations inside the app. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone. Health access is optional.

Notifications

Reapply reminders and daily UV guidance are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Tuuvi learns only whether you have an active subscription or purchase. It never sees your card details, and no payment information reaches us.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers
  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no email addresses or contact details
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone beyond Apple's own services named above — iCloud for syncing, Apple Weather and Apple Maps for the forecast and city name, and the App Store for purchases

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your device will be described in the app's release notes.
