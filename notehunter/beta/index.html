NoteHunter Insiders

Get new versions of NoteHunter before anyone else, tell us what works and what doesn't, and help decide what it becomes. For musicians, students, teachers and anyone learning to hear music.

What you get

  • Every new version early, through Apple's TestFlight app
  • A direct line to the person who builds NoteHunter
  • A free year of NoteHunter once you've sent feedback on two test versions

What we ask

  • Use the test version as you would the real app, for a few sessions
  • Tell us honestly what was confusing, too hard, too easy or broken
  • When a version you tested is released, rate it on the App Store

The next round tests a new way to start: a short check that finds your level and builds your path from there, and reworked lessons where people get stuck most.

How to join

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Open the invite link below on that device and tap Accept, then Install.
  3. To send feedback, take a screenshot in the test app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open NoteHunter in TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Email works too.

Join the beta on TestFlight

The invite link opens soon. Write to hi@tallpoppystudio.com and you'll be among the first in.

Places are limited while the group is small. If the link says the beta is full, write to us and we'll let you know when there's room.

Your data in the beta

Test versions collect the same anonymous data as the released app, described in the privacy policy. Feedback you send through TestFlight also reaches us with your name and email, as TestFlight shows them to the developer. We use them only to reply.
